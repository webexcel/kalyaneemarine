==================About Gayatri Marine Products===================
Explore the Finest Seafood Processing & Export Facilities Worldwide
Gayatri Marine Products is a leading seafood exporter dedicated to delivering premium frozen shrimp worldwide. Established in 2010, our company is strategically located near key aquaculture zones to ensure the freshest and highest-quality seafood. With advanced processing facilities in Oduru, Andhra Pradesh, and decades of industry expertise, we uphold the highest standards of sustainability and excellence. Our commitment to quality has enabled us to serve global markets, including Japan, China, and Vietnam, with top-tier seafood products.
