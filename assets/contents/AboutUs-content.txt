Most Popular Questions
At Gayatri Marine, we value transparency and customer satisfaction. Here are some of the most frequently asked questions to help you understand our processes, quality standards, and global shipping policies.


Where Can I Find Information?
Our website provides detailed information about our sourcing, processing, and export capabilities. For specific inquiries, feel free to contact our support team.

What Are Your Terms And Conditions?
We adhere to strict industry standards, ensuring transparency and compliance with global seafood regulations. Our terms cover order processing, packaging, and delivery details.

Can I Buy Directly From The Factory?
Yes, Gayatri Marine offers direct factory sales for bulk orders, ensuring the best quality at competitive prices. Contact us for bulk purchase inquiries.

We accept multiple payment methods, including wire transfers, LC (Letter of Credit), and other secure international payment options.

When do I receive my order?
Delivery times depend on the destination and order size. We follow a streamlined logistics process to ensure on-time delivery worldwide.
